Delete your account

Mizan AI is developed by Mouad El Jahiri. You can permanently erase your account and everything in it at any time — from inside the app, or by writing to us.

App
Mizan AI (com.mizanai.app)
Effective

01

Delete it yourself, in the app

This is immediate and needs no request:

  1. Open Mizan AI and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all associated data are erased straight away.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. Once it completes the data is gone and cannot be restored by you or by us.

02

Or ask us to do it

If you can no longer sign in, email mizan.ai.dev@gmail.com from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my account.

We reply within 30 days, and in practice much sooner. We may ask you to confirm the request from the account's email address — that is the only way we can be sure the request came from you and not from someone else.

03

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account, permanently:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Every meal, food entry and transcript
  • Your weight history and body measurements
  • Your goals, targets and preferences
  • Your saved meals, streaks and achievements
  • Your profile photo
  • Your usage-analytics records, if you ever turned analytics on

Voice recordings and meal photos are never stored on our servers in the first place. They are processed to read what you said or ate, then discarded.

04

What is kept, and for how long

Two narrow exceptions, neither of which identifies you:

  • Aggregate operating records — counts, timings and service costs with the link to your account removed. These cannot be traced back to you and are kept so we can understand what the service costs to run.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, such as payment records if you ever subscribed. Kept only as long as the law requires, then deleted.

Backups are rotated on a rolling schedule and any residual copy is overwritten within 30 days of deletion.

05

Questions

Write to mizan.ai.dev@gmail.com and a human will reply. See also our privacy policy.
